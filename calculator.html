Pioneer Calc

A free calculator that works like a TI-84 Plus CE: same keys, same menus, same answers. Use it to practice the keystrokes for any of my courses, on a laptop or a phone.

Using your keyboard

Click the screen, then type: digits, + − * / ^ ( ) , and . work as expected. Enter = ENTER, Backspace = DEL, Esc = CLEAR, and the arrow keys move the cursor. On the keyboard, − at the start of an entry types a negative sign; on the calculator keys, use (−) for negatives, just like the real TI-84.

Your lists, stored variables, and settings are saved in this browser, so they're still there when you come back.

Not included (yet)

Graphing (Y=, WINDOW, GRAPH, TABLE), matrices, complex numbers, and programs. Those keys show a short notice instead.
