Using your keyboard

Click the screen, then type: digits, + − * / ^ ( ) and . work as expected. Enter = enter, Backspace = delete, Esc = clear, and the arrow keys move the cursor. On the keyboard, − at the start of an entry types a negative sign; on the calculator keys, use (−) for negatives, just like the real calculator.

Your lists, stored variables, and settings are saved in this browser, so they're still there when you come back.
